Welcome

Hi, it’s nice to meet you! I’m Feng, an MD-PhD working towards a career as a radiologist-statistician. I completed my PhD in the PennSIVE group at the Perelman School of Medicine with Dr. Russell (Taki) Shinohara. I am currently completing a one-year preliminary Internal Medicine residency at Lankenau Medical Center and will be starting as a research-track Diagnostic Radiology resident at the University of Pennsylvania in 2027.

My research interests include developing novel statistical and machine learning methodology for neuroimaging and other big data settings. In neuroimaging, I’ve developed tools for multi-modal image analysis, unsupervised MRI segmentation problems, statistically-robust deep learning image harmonization, and missing-data-aware image segmentation. I also authored a comprehensive review of current statistical and deep learning image harmonization methods. Statistically, I proposed a privacy-preserving algorithm for distributed fitting of population-based reference charts.

As I continue to grow as a radiologist and biostatistician, I’m particularly interested in leveraging powerful and flexible methods, such as deep learning, in statistically-informed ways and building new methods alongside the scientists who will use them. As I learn more about the intricacies of diagnostic radiology, I hope that my methods will not only provide accurate predictions, but also allow for valid inference and new insights for patient-centered care.

Outside of my methodological work, I’m interested in collaborating with biomedical researchers and clinicians to enable them to get the most out of their data in a statistically-sound way, as well as how to interpret studies and clinical trials from a statistical lens. To do so, I’ve served as a statistical collaborator for a number of biomedical, clinical, and health informatics projects.

In this vein, as a graduate student, I founded the Biostatistical Student Analysis Core (BSAC) within the Biostatistics graduate program. BSAC serves to create real-life consulting experience for fellow Biostatistics students and provide an accessible statistical service to fellow biomedical trainees. If you’re a Penn biomedical trainee of any kind and are interested in working with a biostatistician, please reach out through the BSAC Intake Application! We’d love to hear from you!
